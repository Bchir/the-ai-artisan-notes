# Social Syndication Templates

Tested framing models for sharing technical essays on LinkedIn and X.

---

## Template 1: The "We Solved The Wrong Problem" Pattern (LinkedIn)

```markdown
[Contrarian Hook Title]

[1-Sentence Subtitle / Thesis]

We automated [bottleneck X] with AI, but we kept the verification rituals of [era Y].

Here is what broke when we tested it in production:
[2-3 sentences describing the failure mode]

The bottom line:
[TL;DR direct answer]

In my new note for The AI Artisan Notes, I break down:
• [Key shift 1]
• [Key shift 2]
• [Key shift 3]

Full field note here: [Article URL]

How is your team handling this shift?
```

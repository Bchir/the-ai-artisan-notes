Welcome to The AI Artisan Notes: Software Craftsmanship in the Age of AI

October 26, 2026 • 3 min read • By Mohamed Amine Bchir

Welcome to The AI Artisan Notes.

In an era saturated with ephemeral AI demos, buzzwords, and breathless hype cycles, it is easy to lose sight of what truly matters: the craft of software building.

This publication is a dedicated notebook and field log. It documents the hands-on engineering, architectural decisions, and hard-earned lessons that emerge when pairing thoughtful human intellect with modern machine intelligence.

Why "The AI Artisan"?

The word artisan implies care, intentionality, and mastery of one's tools. When we build with artificial intelligence:

  • We treat LLMs as precision instruments, not unpredictable magic boxes.
  • We prioritize determinism, observability, and testability over brittle complexity.
  • We value deep domain context and clean architectures over shallow wrappers.
